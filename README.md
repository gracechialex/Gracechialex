# Hi, I'm Grace Chimezie 👋

**Applied AI & Knowledge Specialist** · Washington, DC

I build practical AI tools for mission-driven organizations. I start with a defined problem, ground the AI in well-organized knowledge, and measure whether it actually works before it scales.

For six years at IREX I've managed knowledge and evidence for global education and leadership programs. Now I'm focused on turning that knowledge into AI tools that staff can trust: tools that cite their sources, respect who can see what, and keep a person in the loop.

---

## 🔭 What I work on

- **Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG):** AI assistants grounded in program reports, with citations and "not found" answers when the evidence isn't there
- **AI agents with human oversight:** multi-step workflows that use approved tools and data, with human approval before anything goes out
- **Knowledge readiness for AI:** metadata, chunking and permissions that make AI answers accurate
- **Evaluation:** test sets and scoring for accuracy, groundedness, safety and cost per query
- **Responsible AI:** least-privilege access, privacy and clear documentation of limits
